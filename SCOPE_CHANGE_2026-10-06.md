# Scope change: public-data-only CHI 2027 submission

**Date:** 2026-10-06 (Asia/Bangkok)  
**Status:** active for the CHI 2027 Poster submission; no new human-participant
activity is authorized by this change.

## Decision

The CHI 2027 submission will be a public-data-only study titled provisionally:

> **What Blind People Dream, Ask, and What AI Tells Them: A Public-Data Study
> of Sensory Mediation in the AI Age**

The previously planned prospective dream-incorporation experiment is removed
from the current submission scope and retained under `future_human_study/` as a
non-deployed future protocol. It is neither cancelled as a scientific idea nor
used to support the current paper.

## New contribution boundary

The current study compares three observable representational spaces:

1. **DreamSpace:** sensory expression in released blind dream reports;
2. **IntentSpace:** information sought in released BLV-originating questions;
3. **MediationSpace:** information selected by human and AI accessibility
   descriptions, including controlled model outputs where reproducibly
   generated.

The directly testable public-data mechanism is:

`BLV-originating question / accessibility instruction -> changed AI output`.

The project may describe this as users *programming the AI-mediated information
environment*. It must not claim that AI programs cognition, alters neural
organization, or changes dreams. DreamSpace is a historical linguistic and
theoretical reference space, not an outcome of AI exposure.

## Consequences for evidence interpretation

- Existing Study 0 outputs remain exploratory historical diagnostics. They do
  not become confirmatory because of this reframing.
- The new public computational study has its own protocol, sample-selection
  rule, model freeze, and analysis plan. No previously viewed outcome is used to
  select its primary result.
- A released human-verified reference dataset is external validation evidence;
  it is not a substitute for claims about all BLV users.
- Public release does not eliminate ethics, licensing, privacy, or
  redistribution review. Gated or noncommercial datasets are never bypassed.

## Implementation gate

No public corpus enters a confirmatory analysis until its source, version,
release terms, fields, and retrieval method are recorded. No new VLM output
enters the analysis until a reproducible model artifact, deterministic prompt,
parameters, compute route, output hashes, and failure policy are frozen before
outcome inspection.
