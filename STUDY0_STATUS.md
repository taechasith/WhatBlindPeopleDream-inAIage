# Study 0 status: public-data sensory audit

**Status:** completed as an exploratory, automated lexical candidate-label audit;
not confirmatory human-coded evidence.

Study 0 compares released DreamBank reports, BLV-originating VizWiz questions,
and a small released set of AI descriptions under the frozen Cognitive Sensing
Ontology. Its candidate-label alignment diagnostics are hypothesis-generating
only because the double-human-coding/reliability gate is still pending and the
AI-description sample is small and heterogeneous.

The observed candidate-label pattern (AI--dream Jensen--Shannon divergence
0.162; AI--BLV-question divergence 0.474) must not be interpreted as cognitive
transfer, an AI effect on dreams, or evidence that AI descriptions enter dreams.
It motivates a distinct prospective experiment (Study 1), whose outcomes have
not been observed.

The primary limitations and all source counts remain in `results/limitations.md`,
`results/alignment_metrics.csv`, and `results/sample_flow.csv`.
