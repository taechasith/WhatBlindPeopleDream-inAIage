# Study 1 dream-report collection protocol

**Protocol status:** non-deployed preparation for BD-AI-CHI27-Study1-v2.0. This document does not authorize recruitment, screening, consent, exposure, recording, or collection of participant data. It becomes usable only after a documented institutional determination/approval and completion of the pre-exposure implementation gates.

## Scope and setting

Study 1 uses four home-based sessions per participant, scheduled 6--8 days apart. The four assigned conditions are DAI, SAI, HUM, and ANC. A participant receives one condition per session through the approved accessible delivery route. The condition, target scene, and allocation order are concealed from dream coders.

The primary outcome concerns the first eligible free dream report after DAI or SAI sessions. This protocol does not induce sleep, use serial awakenings, or ask participants to view the source scene. No target-specific reconstruction, recall, quiz, or marker prompt occurs between the pre-sleep delivery and the first free dream report.

## Pre-sleep session record

Before each approved session, the study system records only the minimum session metadata needed for fidelity and eligibility:

- pseudonymous participant ID and session ID;
- allocated condition and stimulus ID in the restricted allocation system;
- intended and actual start/end times;
- delivery route, TTS/system version, output/transcript hash, and delivery success;
- participant-selected accessibility accommodation;
- a neutral indication of whether the participant intends to sleep after the session; and
- any participant-requested pause, stop, or technical/accessibility deviation.

The participant must not be told the target marker inventory, which items coders will evaluate, or the hypothesis direction. The study material is not navigation, safety, clinical, or therapeutic advice.

## Morning free-report procedure

The first report is participant-initiated after the first main awakening following the study session. The participant records it as soon as reasonably practical and before any target-specific question. The primary report must be received within two hours of final awakening and include enough timing information to evaluate eligibility.

The approved interface offers accessible text entry and, if audio recording is approved, an accessible audio-recording option. The participant chooses the reporting mode. No interviewer is present for the primary home report. Do not record background audio, device activity, location, video, or unrelated conversation.

Use this neutral prompt exactly unless an ethics-approved accessible translation is documented:

> Please describe any dream, thought, feeling, sound, body sensation, place, event, or other experience you remember from sleep. Include only what you remember. There are no right or wrong answers. If you do not remember a dream, you may say: "I do not recall a dream."

A participant may optionally receive one neutral follow-up only:

> Is there anything else you remember?

Do not mention the scene, image, AI, study material, target objects, spatial relations, decoy units, or expected dream incorporation before the first free report.

## Multiple reports and post-report activities

If a participant provides multiple spontaneous reports after one session, retain each with its timestamp. The first eligible free report is primary; later reports are retained for prespecified exploratory sensitivity analyses. Do not combine reports without preserving their order and source.

A delayed reconstruction or rating task, if retained in the ethics-approved protocol, occurs only after the first free report and all neutral follow-up. It is secondary, never an immediate pre-sleep measure, and cannot alter eligibility of the already collected primary report.

## Eligibility and missingness

A primary report is eligible only when all of the following are documented:

1. the participant confirms a sleep episode after the assigned session;
2. the report is free rather than target-cued;
3. the report contains at least one describable dream/mentation element;
4. timing is verifiable;
5. the audio/text record is usable for approved transcription; and
6. no wrong stimulus, unrecoverable delivery failure, or material protocol deviation is identified.

No dream recall, no submitted report, no sleep, unusable record, unverified timing, or an ineligible report is missing data, not evidence of zero incorporation. Preserve the reason code. Do not impute dream-incorporation values.

## Accessibility, language, and transcription

All collection instructions and controls must be screen-reader-compatible, keyboard-operable, available in the participant's approved preferred format, and usable with pause/repeat controls. The study language and any accessible translation must be frozen before recruitment. Preserve the original-language report. Any transcription or translation workflow must be approved, versioned, quality-checked, and kept separate from condition/allocation data.

If speech-to-text assistance is used, retain the original approved audio under restricted access, document the tool/version, and conduct the approved human quality check without exposing condition or target status to coders.

## Privacy, storage, and withdrawal

Use a random study ID. Keep consent/contact records, allocation records, raw audio, transcripts, and analysis files in separate access-controlled locations. The approved storage provider, encryption, role permissions, retention period, deletion window, backup policy, and lawful limits on deletion must be communicated in final consent materials.

Participants may skip, pause, stop, decline a report, or withdraw without penalty. The final consent form must state how already supplied recordings, transcripts, and de-identified analysis records are handled after withdrawal.

## Deviations, distress, and incidents

Stop the session if a participant reports distress, fatigue, inability to access the system, or a wish to stop. Follow the approved distress and adverse-event procedures; do not provide dream interpretation or clinical counseling. Record only the minimum necessary incident/deviation information in restricted records. Never alter or discard a report silently; preserve an eligibility/deviation record and apply the preregistered analysis rule.
