# Level 2 reconstruction-measurement validity

## Threat

An explicit reconstruction task immediately after a pre-sleep description would
rehearse the target semantic/spatial content. Even when given in every condition,
that rehearsal could increase target salience and change the primary dream-
incorporation outcome. It would make the overnight study estimate exposure plus
test-induced rehearsal rather than the intended pre-sleep information effect.

## Prospective decision

The main overnight Study 1 **will not administer an explicit semantic or spatial
reconstruction test between exposure and sleep**. Thus immediate reconstruction
is not a main-study confirmatory outcome.

The first post-sleep free dream report remains primary. A short delayed
reconstruction task may occur only after that report and after all neutral dream
prompts; it is a secondary post-sleep representation outcome and cannot be
called an immediate effect. It must not contain target cues before free report.

If an immediate mental/spatial reconstruction question is scientifically needed,
it requires a separate, prospectively randomized non-overnight substudy or
separate sessions whose results are not pooled with the primary dream outcome.

## Implications

- The primary overnight estimand is protected from deliberate task rehearsal.
- Ratings of burden, agency, source recognition, and general understanding may
  be collected with non-target-specific items before sleep; they are delivery
  checks, not reconstruction tests.
- This reduces the scope of RQ3. Any future immediate-representation claim
  requires its own pre-specified design.
