# Future matched-human validation archive

**Status:** historical methodology only; not part of the current CHI 2027
public-data computational study.

On 2026-10-09, the current study was amended to collect zero new human data.
The former workflow requiring three independent human coders, adjudication, and
the Alternative Annotator Test (Alt-Test) is preserved here for a later,
separately approved matched-human validation or full-paper extension. It must
not be used to block the current `CODED_DATA_FREEZE`.

The archived workflow was designed to test a different question: whether a
synthetic annotation instrument can be compared with same-item human coding.
The current study makes no such equivalence claim. Its active policy is
`ANNOTATION_VALIDATION_POLICY.md`; its active construct-tier decisions are in
`CONSTRUCT_MEASUREMENT_TIER_MATRIX.csv`.

Public historical source files are snapshotted under this directory. Local
private packets, any future returned coder files, and condition mappings remain
outside version control under `future_human_validation/private/` or their
original protected locations. `ARCHIVE_MANIFEST_2026-10-09.csv` records the
source version and reason for preservation.
