# Study 1 stimulus audit

Four non-identifying, original vector **v2** prototypes are registered in
`STUDY1_STIMULI.csv` and `stimuli/`. The v1 assets were rejected after local
render inspection because their abstraction did not visibly support their
marker inventories. Every v2 set has eight distinctive target
semantic units and is paired with a different eight-unit foil set. Scenes avoid
trauma, sex, medical content, famous locations, identifiable people, and private
environments. Their intended affect is neutral to mildly pleasant/alert; it is
not a manipulation of fear or threat.

The current assets are suitable for non-human design review, not participant
exposure. Before ethics submission, an independent accessibility and cultural
review must check familiarity, wording, marker distinctiveness, unintended
hazards, and whether any object/relation imposes a sighted ontology. Before
participant exposure, a frozen AI system must generate/audit final DAI/SAI
outputs and a qualified human author must independently prepare final HUM/ANC
audio. No generated output may make unsupported identity, emotion, demographic,
relationship, or safety claims.

Candidate controls are audited for equal eight-unit marker inventory,
neutral/mild affect band, moderate visual complexity, and common-object
familiarity. Static DAI/HUM/ANC audio is planned for a brief (under 90-second)
delivery followed by a common quiet period within a 12-minute session envelope.
Exact word count, audio duration, lexical complexity, and content fidelity are
`PENDING` until final scripts/TTS exist; they are required manipulation checks,
not silently assumed matches. The longer participant-steered SAI interaction is
intentionally a package-level condition, not a text-duration-matched control.
