# Washout and carryover rationale

## Decision

Use one session per calendar week, with an allowed 6--8 day interval. This is a
conservative, burden-aware design constraint rather than a claim that memory or
dream material disappears exactly after seven days.

## Rationale

Dream incorporation and memory reprocessing can occur at variable delays, and
repeated pre-sleep content can carry over. The study therefore separates
different scene/marker sets across sessions, counterbalances period/order, logs
actual session dates and sleep timing, and includes carryover/first-session-only
sensitivity analyses. A shorter interval would increase practical burden from
overlap; a longer one would increase attrition without proving absence of
carryover.

## Pre-specified handling

- A session outside 6--8 days is recorded as a protocol deviation, retained,
  and excluded only in the pre-specified deviation sensitivity analysis.
- A missed session is rescheduled once within the window where possible; no
  condition or stimulus is substituted and no extra session is added.
- A participant with a report that explicitly references a prior study scene is
  flagged for exploratory carryover coding; it is not deleted from the primary
  dataset.
- Models include period/order and a sensitivity analysis excluding any flagged
  carryover report.

## Evidence boundary

This rationale draws on the established possibility of narrative/learning
incorporation into dream reports; it does not assume a universal or fixed
"dream-lag" duration. The final preregistration cites the verified dream-
incorporation literature in `STUDY1_EVIDENCE_MAP.csv`.
