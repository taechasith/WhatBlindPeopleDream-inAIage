# Draft accessibility accommodations plan

All study interaction must be usable with screen readers and keyboard-only
navigation. Provide accessible text and audio, adjustable speech rate, pause and
repeat controls, a plain-language alternative, and the participant's preferred
reporting mode. Do not require image viewing. Offer a technology check before a
session without collecting outcome data. Record only the accommodation needed to
operate the study, not an inferred functional diagnosis. Any support person is
chosen by the participant and must not be pressured to hear dream content.
