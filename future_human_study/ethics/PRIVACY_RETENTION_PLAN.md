# Draft privacy, recording, and retention plan

Raw dream audio/transcripts, AI transcripts, session timing, and optional
demographics are sensitive research records. Store them in access-controlled,
encrypted institutional storage separate from the participant-contact key. Use a
random study ID in analysis files. No source images, raw dream reports, or
identifiable recordings are released by default.

Record only what is needed: delivery logs, consent status, accessible-format
preference, session timing, free dream report, and only any delayed post-report
secondary response explicitly approved before collection. Do not collect an
immediate/pre-sleep reconstruction measure, precise location, contacts, device
contents, or private images. The retention duration, deletion window, backup
policy, access roles, and any lawful limits on withdrawal must be set by the
responsible institution before consent documents are finalized.
