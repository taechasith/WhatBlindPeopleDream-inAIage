# Stimulus codebook

Each prototype has eight preregistered semantic units. A unit may be an object,
an action, or a relational proposition. The unit inventory is used for output
audit and blinded target/decoy scoring, not for prompting participants.

The original `S01`--`S04` vector files are retained as rejected, overly abstract
design history. Only their `*_v2.svg` successors are candidates for later
grounding review; they remain `NOT_FOR_EXPOSURE` until the model-output, TTS,
and independent review gates pass.

## Inclusion rules

- An object unit needs a distinctive, visible entity.
- A relational unit needs both referents and a direction/location relation.
- An action unit must be visually represented rather than inferred intention.
- Auditory/tactile associations are design annotations only; they are never
  presented as properties unless actually delivered by the final output.

## Exclusion rules

- Do not add identity, age, gender, relationship, emotion, ownership, or motive
  units without visual evidence and a specific study need.
- Do not create real-world safety instructions from a static prototype scene.
- Do not treat a generic category (for example, "thing" or "place") as a
  semantic target.

## Decoy rules

Each target stimulus maps to a matched, unexposed partner. Decoys must not share
the same distinctive object or relation as their target set. Sets are randomly
relabeled for coders. Any decoy leakage into final audio/output disqualifies that
unit from primary target-versus-decoy scoring.
