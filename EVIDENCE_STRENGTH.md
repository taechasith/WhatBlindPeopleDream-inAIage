# Phase 2 evidence-strength classification

| Class | Meaning | Records |
|---|---|---|
| Direct observational dream evidence | Reports sensory or dream outcomes in defined blind groups; no causal AI inference | KANG2023; MEAIDI2014 |
| Method/review context | Establishes measurement or conceptual constraints, not a primary outcome for this study | ILIC2023; VANDERHEIJDEN2024 |
| Protocol only | Describes planned work and must not support outcome claims | BLINDREAM2025 |
| Direct AI-accessibility evidence | User study, released corpus, or evaluation of AI-mediated information | KREISS2022; MOHANBABU2024; XIE2025; KARMOLEGKOU2025; GUIDEDOG2026 |
| Reliability-signaling comparator | Tests explanation-quality signaling under inability to see visual context; not a dream or longitudinal study | HE2026 |

## He et al. (2026) scope assessment

- **Epistemic autonomy:** Directly relevant: scores provide reliability
  information rather than only a persuasive explanation.
- **Uncertainty/reliability signaling:** Directly relevant: Visual Fidelity and
  Contrastiveness operationalize explanation reliability/calibration.
- **User overreliance:** Directly relevant to its stated task; reduced false
  belief is not a general clinical or real-world safety outcome.
- **Visual-context inaccessibility:** Directly relevant; participants judged
  predictions without seeing input, including evaluation on VizWiz.
- **AI-mediated sensory information:** Indirect but useful: this is about
  faithful communication of visual evidence, not experienced sensory modalities.
- **Reciprocal Cognitive Sensing Loop:** Informs the observable AI-to-human
  information-environment branch only; it does not test human-to-AI grounding,
  blind dreams, or a long-term cognitive pathway.
