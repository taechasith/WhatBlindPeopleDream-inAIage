# Consent form -- draft for ethics review only

Do not use this document for enrollment before the required institutional
determination/approval.

- I have read or had explained the participant information sheet in an
  accessible format.
- I understand that the study includes four home-based pre-sleep sessions, each
  6--8 days apart, and optional free dream reports after waking.
- I understand that I will receive each of four approved study-material types:
  default AI description, participant-steered AI interaction, human-authored
  reference description, and neutral audio narrative. Their order is assigned
  in advance.
- I understand that no target-specific reconstruction test occurs before sleep.
  Any approved post-report task occurs only after my first free dream report and
  will be described in the final information sheet.
- I understand that I may report no dream recall and may skip any content.
- I understand that AI output can be inaccurate and is not real-world safety or
  navigation guidance.
- I understand the recording, privacy, withdrawal, and retention arrangements
  that will be completed after ethics review. I may choose the approved text or
  audio reporting option and may decline to make a report.
- I understand I can stop participation without penalty and can state a data
  handling preference for records already provided, subject to applicable law.
- I voluntarily agree to participate.

Participant name/signature/date, authorized consent process, contact details,
compensation, retention period, institutional privacy notice, and ethics approval
reference are intentionally **PENDING** and must be inserted only after approval.
