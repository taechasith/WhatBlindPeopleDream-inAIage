# Draft AI disclosure for ethics review only

You will receive information about a scene through a computer system that uses
artificial intelligence in some sessions. The system can make mistakes, omit
important information, or express uncertainty poorly. It is not a navigation,
safety, medical, legal, or emergency tool. Do not use it to make real-world
safety decisions. The scenes are study materials, not your surroundings.

The study records the system version, prompts, and outputs for research-quality
checks. You may ask for clarification within the session limits. We will not
claim the system is accurate simply because it sounds confident.
