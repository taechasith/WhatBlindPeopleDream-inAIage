# Withdrawal instructions — draft for ethics review only

You may stop a session immediately, skip a question, decline to record a dream,
or withdraw from future sessions without giving a reason. Before enrollment, the
approved consent process will explain how to contact the study team, whether
already de-identified records can still be deleted, and any legal or scientific
limits on removal after analysis or publication. Payment for completed work must
follow the ethics-approved prorating policy and must not depend on dream content.
