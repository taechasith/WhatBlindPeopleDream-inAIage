# Participant information sheet -- draft for ethics review only

**Do not distribute, recruit with, or use this draft for consent.** The study is
not submitted for ethics review and no participant activity may begin until the
applicable institutional determination/approval is documented.

## Why is this study being done?

This study examines how people describe dreams after receiving accessible
information before sleep. Some sessions use an AI system and others use matched
audio materials. We are studying information and dream reports, not diagnosing
dreams, changing dreams, or measuring brain change.

## What would happen?

If you choose to participate after the study receives the required ethics
determination/approval, you may
complete four home sessions, each 6--8 days apart. At each session, before sleep,
you would receive one accessible study material within a short, fixed session
window: a default AI scene description, an interaction in which you can ask the
approved AI system questions about a scene, a human-authored reference
description, or a neutral audio passage. The order is assigned in advance so
that each participant receives all four kinds of material.

There is no test asking you to reconstruct the scene before sleep. After waking,
you may record any dream you remember using an accessible text or audio option.
You may say that you do not recall a dream. You will not be asked target-specific
questions before your first free report. If an approved version includes a short
post-report task, it occurs only after that free report and will be described in
the final consent materials.

## Possible risks and benefits

Thinking about sleep or dreams can feel tiring or upsetting. AI information can
be incomplete or wrong, and descriptions may not match every person's preferred
way of understanding a scene. Study materials are not navigation or safety
advice. There is no promised personal benefit, no promise that you will dream
about the material, and no claim that the study changes cognition or the brain.

## Choice, privacy, and accessibility

Participation is voluntary. You may skip questions, pause, stop, or withdraw
without penalty. We will provide materials in accessible formats and ask about
your preferred accommodations. Dream reports and recordings can contain personal
information; the final approved consent form will explain access controls,
retention, deletion limits, compensation, contacts, and the handling of records
already provided. This draft does not yet specify the institution, contacts,
payment, or retention period and cannot be used before the required ethics
determination/approval.
