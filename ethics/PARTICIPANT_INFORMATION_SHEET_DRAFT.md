# Participant information sheet — draft for ethics review only

## Why is this study being done?

This study examines how people describe dreams after receiving accessible
information about a scene before sleep. Some sessions use an AI system and some
use other matched audio materials. We are studying information and dream reports,
not diagnosing dreams or measuring brain change.

## What would happen?

If you choose to participate after the study receives ethics approval, you may
complete up to four short home sessions about one week apart. Before sleep, you
would listen to or interact with accessible study material, complete a brief
understanding task, and record any dream you recall after waking. You can report
that you do not recall a dream. The study team will give final time estimates
only after pilot-free materials and accessibility testing are approved.

## Possible risks and benefits

Thinking about sleep or dreams can feel tiring or upsetting. AI information can
be incomplete or wrong. Study materials are not navigation or safety advice.
There is no promised personal benefit, and no promise that you will dream about
the material.

## Choice, privacy, and accessibility

Participation is voluntary. You may skip questions, pause, stop, or withdraw
without penalty. We will provide materials in accessible formats and ask about
your preferred accommodations. Dream reports and recordings can contain personal
information and will be protected as described in the final approved consent
form. This draft does not yet specify the institution, contacts, payment, or
retention period; ethics approval is required before use.
