# Recruitment text -- draft for ethics review only

Adults who identify as blind or low-vision may be invited to consider a future
research study about accessible pre-sleep information and dream reporting. The
planned study involves four home sessions, 6--8 days apart, using accessible
AI-mediated and non-AI audio materials, followed by optional free dream reports.
The study has not begun recruitment and will proceed only after institutional
ethics determination/approval. Eligibility, compensation, contact details, and
the final time commitment remain pending that approval.

The study will not promise dream induction, improved cognition, brain change, or
accurate AI guidance. Accessible information formats and accommodations will be
available. This draft must not be posted, distributed, or used to contact anyone.
