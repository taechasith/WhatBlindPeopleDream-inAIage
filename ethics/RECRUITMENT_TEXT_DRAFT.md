# Recruitment text — draft for ethics review only

Adults who identify as blind or low-vision may be invited to consider a future
research study about accessible pre-sleep information and dream reporting. The
study has not begun recruitment and will proceed only after institutional ethics
approval. Participation, eligibility, compensation, locations, contact details,
and final time commitment are pending that approval.

The study will not promise dream induction, improved cognition, or accurate AI
guidance. Accessible information formats and accommodations will be available.
