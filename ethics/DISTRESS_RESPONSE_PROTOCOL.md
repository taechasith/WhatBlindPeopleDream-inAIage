# Draft dream-content sensitivity and distress response protocol

This document is not yet deployed. Staff must receive ethics-approved training
before use.

- Before each session, remind participants that they can skip, pause, stop, or
  withdraw without penalty; they need not describe upsetting content.
- If distress, fatigue, anxiety, a nightmare concern, or accessibility failure
  is expressed, stop the session, ask only whether immediate assistance is
  wanted, and follow the approved local safety/referral path.
- Do not provide clinical interpretation, dream analysis, or crisis counseling
  unless staff are separately qualified and the ethics-approved plan permits it.
- Document only the minimum necessary incident information in a restricted log;
  follow the ethics body's adverse-event reporting requirements.
- If a participant reports an imminent risk of harm, follow the approved local
  emergency procedure rather than attempting research follow-up.
