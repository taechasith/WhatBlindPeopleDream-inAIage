# Draft recording policy

If approved, participants may choose to submit a spoken dream report or an
accessible text report. Record only the report they elect to provide; do not
record background audio, device activity, camera video, location, or unrelated
conversation. The participant must be told before recording begins, be able to
pause/redo a recording, and receive an accessible confirmation of successful
upload. Audio is transcribed under controlled access; raw audio and transcripts
are linked only by study ID. The final approved policy must specify retention,
transcription vendor/staff, quality assurance, access roles, and deletion limits.
