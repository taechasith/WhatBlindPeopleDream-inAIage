# Study 1 control-condition audit

## Finding

The v1.0 matched false-source control was inadequate as an experimental control.
An undisclosed scene/marker set can establish a blinded coding false-positive
baseline, but it does not balance audio exposure, semantic richness, speech
duration, lexical complexity, emotional salience, spatial detail, interaction,
or expectation. It cannot isolate an AI-mediated information effect.

## Amendment

The design now retains **false-source foil units** as a negative coding control
and adds two exposure controls: HUM (matched human reference description) and
ANC (matched active neutral narrative). This separates source specificity from
the more general effects of receiving pre-sleep audio/narrative content.

| Dimension | False-source foil only | v1.1 design control |
|---|---|---|
| Source identity | Not delivered; cannot compare delivery source | DAI vs HUM contrasts source/output provenance; disclosure is explicit. |
| Target semantic information | Absent by design | Scene sets carry matched marker inventories; ANC has no target markers. |
| Description length / speech duration | Unmatched | Same TTS and 12-minute envelope; word/TTS duration logged and adjusted. |
| Lexical complexity | Unmatched | Candidate descriptions are pre-audited; readability/word statistics frozen. |
| Emotional salience | Unmatched | Neutral-to-mild scenes/narrative matched using a preregistered rubric. |
| Spatial detail | Unmatched | DAI/HUM scene descriptions contain matched relation inventory; ANC contains no target-scene relation. |
| Unique markers | Unmatched | Eight target and eight foil semantic units per scene set, independently audited. |
| Sleep timing | Unmatched | Same 30 +/- 10 minute pre-sleep timing target, recorded. |
| Interaction time | Unmatched | Fixed 12-minute envelope; SAI duration and question categories logged. |
| Agency | Unmatched | SAI intentionally differs; agency is a manipulation check/secondary outcome, not treated as a nuisance. |
| Expectation/placebo | Unmatched | Every night includes accessible pre-sleep audio and the same reporting instruction; source disclosure prevents unethical deception but residual expectation is measured and reported. |

## Residual limitations

SAI cannot be fully length- or content-matched to DAI without eliminating its
interactivity. Its causal estimand is therefore the effect of a participant-led
information interaction, inclusive of its content and agency differences—not a
pure linguistic-style effect. The primary target-versus-foil test is robust to
this distinction; the DAI-versus-SAI contrast is secondary.

The active controls do not prove that an observed effect is unique to AI rather
than meaningful pre-sleep language generally. That question requires the
preregistered contrasts and cautious interpretation.
